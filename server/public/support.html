Alumni Watch Support

Running into a bug, have a feature request, or need help with your account or subscription? Email joel.wyngarden@gmail.com and you'll get a reply directly from the developer.

Common questions:

  • I want to add more than 3 players. Open My Roster and tap "Go unlimited" to subscribe, or "Restore purchase" if you've already subscribed on another device.
  • I want to delete my account and data. Email the address above from your account's email (or note your Apple sign-in relay address) and it will be removed within 30 days. See the privacy policy for details.
  • My subscription isn't showing as active. Try "Restore purchase" from My Roster first — if that doesn't fix it, email us with the Apple ID email you subscribed with.
